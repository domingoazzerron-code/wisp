The 4 D’s
Delay

Most cravings fade in 5 to 10 minutes. Start the timer on Today.

Deep breaths

In for 4 seconds, hold for 4, out for 6. Repeat five times.

Drink water

Sip a full glass slowly. It keeps your hands and mouth busy.

Do something else

Take a short walk, wash your face, chew gum, or message a friend.

Support makes it easier
Talk to a professional

A doctor or pharmacist can go through nicotine replacement and other options, which improve your chances of quitting.

Try a quitline

Many countries run a free quitline. Search for the one in your area.

Tell someone

One friend who knows your plan can make a hard day easier.

Common triggers
After meals

Brush your teeth right after eating, or leave the table.

Coffee

Switch to tea for a week, or change when you have your coffee.

Stress

Step outside for a minute and breathe before you decide.

Social

Tell friends you are cutting down and ask them not to offer.
